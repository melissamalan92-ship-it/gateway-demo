Our free shuttle runs **every hour from 7am to 9pm, Monday to Friday**, between the residence and campus.

## Where it goes

It covers Braamfontein, Doornfontein and Auckland Park, and can take students as far as Soweto and Sandton.

## Good to know

- The shuttle is free for all Gateway residents.
- Times may change during exams and holidays. We'll post any changes here.
